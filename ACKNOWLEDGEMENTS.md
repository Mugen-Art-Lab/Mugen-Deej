# Acknowledgements

Mugen Deej exists because the original [deej](https://github.com/omriharel/deej) project by Omri Harel demonstrated how useful and approachable a physical Arduino volume mixer can be.

Mugen Deej is an independent Windows client built around a compatible hardware idea and serial protocol, with its own interface, connection handling, diagnostics, audio-device support, and reference firmware. It is not an official continuation of the original project.

Thank you to Omri Harel and everyone who contributed to the deej community.
